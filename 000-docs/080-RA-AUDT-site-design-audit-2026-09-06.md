## Headline

The homepage ships every item on the brief's remove list and states three contradictory stat sets on one scroll, two of which overstate the verified numbers. Retired Google infrastructure is still the page's identity (tech strip, service line, default meta description, five pages). Eighteen of thirty routes sit outside the brief's information architecture; eleven components have no importer but still hold banned copy, including the fabricated testimonials. The visual system is the anti-slop cluster the `frontend-design` skill names (near-black, one accent, star field, cosmic backdrop) and ships framer-motion, gsap, lenis, and a React hydration bundle on a page with no interaction. There are zero spec files, two dead Playwright configs, a lockfile out of sync with package.json, and one Umami event. The repo CLAUDE.md instructs future agents to reintroduce the stale counts and the booking CTA.
