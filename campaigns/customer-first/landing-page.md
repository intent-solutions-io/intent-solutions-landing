# Copy rationale

Vibe Marketing Direct Response Copy, using the owner-approved positioning and existing House voice. Impeccable preserves the existing identity and makes the hierarchy readable. This is a compact company gateway, not a long-form sales page. No artificial urgency, invented savings, new price menu, or unready newsletter signup.

Editorial baseline score, not conversion research: clarity 6, specificity 6, voice 7, desire 5, proof 5, urgency 1, flow 5 (35/70). The weak points were technical language before customer purpose, repeated network routing, and described evidence without an in-page specimen. Urgency remains intentionally low. Do not manufacture a deadline to improve a score.

## Control: customer-first

Bring us the work you need AI to do.

Intent Solutions builds and operates AI systems around a defined business outcome. We work out what must change, how to test it, and who will be responsible when it runs.

Start with the problem. You do not need a model or a technical specification picked out.

Request an outcome. Inspect the work.

What does done look like? A task your team needs completed, the information it depends on, and what must stay under human control. A way to check the result, including what failed. Ownership, monitoring, and a response when something goes wrong.

See what ran. Check what happened. A demo shows behavior. A test checks a claim. Our public work gives you both to inspect before you decide to work with us. It is evidence of our method, not a promise about your result. A dated failed Labs gate stays visible with its definition and timestamp. The record describes one gate, not an entire system.

The tools can change. The discipline stays. Frame the work, build the system, test the claim, and establish how it will operate. The model follows the task and the constraints.

A team stands behind the work. Read about the people and their responsibilities. Practitioners can explore Learn separately; tools and examples live in Projects.

What work needs to change? Tell us what your team does today and what a useful result would look like. We review the fit before proposing the work. Request an outcome.

The rendered Astro homepage is the exact public copy authority, including its evidence links and method detail.

## Challenger: evidence-first, not deployed

Before you trust the result, inspect the work.

An AI system can produce a confident answer and still fail the task. Start with something you can check: what ran, what it was tested against, and what happened.

Intent Solutions builds and operates AI systems around defined business outcomes. We publish examples of our evaluation work so you can examine the method before starting a conversation. A public test result is evidence about that test, not a guarantee for your business.

Inspect the work. Request an outcome.

Our Labs records keep failed decisions visible. Evals explains the definitions behind them. Demos lets you explore systems and experiments, while the public repositories show the implementation and contribution history. Each surface answers a different question; you do not need to visit all of them to send a request.

Bring a task your team needs completed. We define what success means, what information the system can use, and where human approval belongs. We build around those constraints and test the result. Operating responsibility, monitoring, and recovery belong in the plan too.

The team and its responsibilities are public on the About page. Existing customers follow their agreed support path. Practitioners who want to learn the method can explore the separate Learn practice and its access requirements.

Tell us what must change and how you would recognize a useful result. We review the request before proposing the work. Request an outcome.
