# Accountable AI implementation

Approved by the owner on 2026-09-12 following the positioning recommendation.

Intent Solutions starts with the business work a customer needs done, defines how to check the outcome, and establishes responsibility for operating the system. Evidence supports this positioning; practitioner access is a secondary path, not the main customer offer.

Primary headline: Bring us the work you need AI to do.

Use the existing House register in Intent OS: plain language, specific mechanisms, honest limits, no invented metrics, urgency, customer endorsements, or em/en dashes. Company copy uses we; do not transplant personal biography into sales copy. Existing orange/Syne/Inter identity stays.

Audience assumption: business decision-makers who know they want AI help but have not selected an implementation approach. Technical evaluators need evidence. Practitioners use Learn. Validate these assumptions from qualified requests, not pageviews alone.

Competitive scan on 2026-09-12: HatchWorks emphasizes outcomes and accountability (https://hatchworks.com/); Thoughtworks emphasizes production and governance (https://www.thoughtworks.com/what-we-do/enterprise-ai). Those claims are not unique to Intent Solutions. Differentiate through linked artifacts and clear responsibility, not exclusivity claims.

Alternatives considered: evidence-first for technical readers; practitioner-first for recruiting builders. Neither displaces the customer as the company's primary homepage audience.
